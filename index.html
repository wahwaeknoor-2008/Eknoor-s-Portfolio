Method04 / 07

How I work

  1. 01

    Understand

    I start from what the person needs. For client websites, that means turning requirements into a clear design they can use.

  2. 02

    Build

    I build the working version end to end, then connect the other pieces: Arduino sensors, FastAPI endpoints or a React frontend.

  3. 03

    Test and debug

    I test each feature and fix what breaks. Resumind and my Arduino builds both went through repeated rounds of this.

  4. 04

    Ship

    I put the work in front of real users. Resumind is live, and I keep maintaining the websites I build for clients.
