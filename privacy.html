Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what personal information eknoorwadhwa.com (“this site”) collects, why, and what I do with it. The site is my personal portfolio. I am Eknoor Wadhwa, an individual, and I run it myself.

1. What I collect

Contact form

If you use the contact form, I receive the name, email address and message you type in. Nothing else is required.

Direct contact

If you email or phone me, I receive whatever you choose to send, along with your email address or phone number.

Technical data from third parties

This site has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. Some technical data still reaches the services that deliver the site:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.) hosts this site and may log your IP address and request details.
  • Google Fonts (Google) delivers the fonts used on the site, so Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads.
  • GitHub also serves the contribution graph image in the GitHub section, so it receives your IP address when that image loads.

Stored on your device

If you switch between light and dark mode, the site saves your choice in your browser’s local storage under the name eknoor-theme. It stays on your device, is not a cookie and is never sent to me.

2. How I use it

I use contact details and messages only to read and reply to you, and to keep a record of the conversation. I do not sell your information, add you to mailing lists or use it for advertising or automated decision-making.

3. Who else handles it

Contact form submissions are forwarded to my University of Waterloo email address by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service. FormSubmit processes your submission to deliver it. Messages then sit in my University of Waterloo email account, which is subject to the University’s own policies. I do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires it.

4. How long I keep it

I keep messages for as long as I need them to respond and to keep a record of the correspondence. You can ask me to delete them at any time.

5. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information I hold about you by emailing e4wadhwa@uwaterloo.ca. I will respond within a reasonable time. You can also use the site without submitting the form, and your browser lets you clear local storage at any time.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS and I limit what it collects. No online service is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information through the contact form.

7. Other websites

This site links to other websites, including GitHub, LinkedIn, Vercel and itch.io. They have their own privacy practices and this policy does not cover them.

8. Children

This site is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes

If I change this policy, I will update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy: e4wadhwa@uwaterloo.ca.
